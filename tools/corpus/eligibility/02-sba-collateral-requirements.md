# SBA 7(a) Loan Collateral Requirements
## Source: SBA SOP 50 10 v8.1 (June 2025)

## Collateral Policy Overview

Collateral is required for all SBA 7(a) loans over $50,000. Lenders must follow a collateral-taking policy that is commercially reasonable and consistent with safe and sound lending practices. SBA does not decline a loan solely because it is not fully collateralized, but adequate collateral strengthens the application.

## Collateral Threshold

- **Loans of $50,000 or less**: collateral not required by SBA policy (lender may impose its own requirement)
- **Loans over $50,000 up to $500,000**: collateral required; lender must take all available business assets; lender should also take personal assets of owners with 20%+ equity if business assets are insufficient
- **Loans over $500,000**: full collateralization required to the extent possible; both business and personal real estate must be offered if available

## Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratios by Collateral Type

Lenders apply the following maximum LTV limits when calculating collateral coverage:

| Collateral Type | Maximum LTV |
|---|---|
| Commercial real estate (owner-occupied) | 90% of appraised value |
| Commercial real estate (non-owner-occupied) | 75% of appraised value |
| Residential real estate | 85% of appraised value |
| Equipment (new) | 75% of purchase price |
| Equipment (used) | 50% of orderly liquidation value |
| Inventory | 25–50% of book value |
| Accounts receivable (eligible, < 90 days) | 75% of face value |
| Accounts receivable (ineligible or > 90 days) | 0% |
| Leasehold improvements | 0% (considered unsecured) |
| Intellectual property / goodwill | 0% (not accepted as collateral) |

## Loan-to-Revenue Ratio Policy

In addition to LTV, lenders assess the loan-to-revenue ratio:

- **Loan amount must not exceed 50% of trailing 12-month gross annual revenue** for most 7(a) loans
- Loans where the requested amount exceeds 75% of annual revenue are flagged as **HIGH RISK** and require additional underwriting scrutiny
- Loans where the requested amount exceeds 100% of annual revenue are generally considered **INELIGIBLE** without exceptional compensating factors (e.g., strong collateral, DSCR > 2.0x, established 10+ year operating history)

## Personal Guarantee Requirements

- **All owners with 20% or more equity** must provide an unlimited personal guarantee
- Spouses of 20%+ equity owners must also provide a limited personal guarantee (up to their ownership interest in joint marital assets)
- A partial personal guarantee (limited) may be accepted from owners with 5–19% equity interest at lender discretion

## Collateral Documentation

Required collateral documents:
- UCC-1 Financing Statement (for personal property)
- Deed of Trust or Mortgage (for real estate)
- Current appraisal (within 12 months for real estate, equipment appraisal for assets > $250,000)
- Insurance certificates naming SBA and lender as loss payees
