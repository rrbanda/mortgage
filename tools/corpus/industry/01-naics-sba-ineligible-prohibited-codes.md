## SBA-Prohibited NAICS Codes (13 CFR § 120.110)

The following NAICS codes correspond to business types that are categorically ineligible for SBA 7(a) loan programs. When an applicant's primary NAICS code falls in this list, the application must be declined as INELIGIBLE without proceeding to financial underwriting. Document the specific CFR clause in the decline reason.

NAICS codes 111998 (industrial hemp, limited exception) and 459991 are commonly used; evaluate the actual product/service to determine federal legality.

## High-Risk Industries (Require Additional Underwriting Scrutiny)

These industries are eligible for SBA loans but carry elevated default risk based on historical SBA loan performance data and FDIC community bank lending surveys. Applications from these industries require enhanced underwriting review, stronger compensating factors, and documented risk mitigation.

| NAICS Code | Industry | Risk Category | Primary Underwriting Concern |
|---|---|---|---|
| 711310 | Promoters of Performing Arts, Sports, and Similar Events | ELEVATED RISK | Revenue is event-dependent and highly seasonal; no recurring revenue base; vulnerable to economic downturns |
| 711320 | Promoters of Performing Arts Without Facilities | ELEVATED RISK | As above; no owned real estate or equipment as collateral anchor |
| 561920 | Convention and Trade Show Organizers | ELEVATED RISK | Revenue concentrated in events; COVID-type disruptions can eliminate revenue entirely; typically no hard asset collateral |
| 561990 | All Other Support Services (event-related) | ELEVATED RISK | Broad category; verify specific business activity; event-adjacent businesses inherit high revenue volatility |
| 722511 | Full-Service Restaurants | ELEVATED RISK | ~60% failure rate within first year; cash-intensive operations; thin margins (3–9% net); lease-dependent; high labor cost exposure |
| 722513 | Limited-Service Restaurants (fast food, QSR) | MODERATE-ELEVATED | Lower failure rate than full-service; franchise models reduce risk significantly; assess royalty obligations |
| 722410 | Drinking Places (Alcoholic Beverages) — Bars/Taverns | ELEVATED RISK | Highly regulated (liquor license risk); dependent on foot traffic; low asset base; revenue volatility |
| 485310 | Taxi and Rideshare Services | ELEVATED RISK | Disrupted by technology platforms; vehicle asset depreciation; high insurance costs; driver turnover |
| 441120 | Used Motor Vehicle Dealers | ELEVATED RISK | Inventory financing risk; floor plan exposure; subject to regulatory scrutiny; revenue tied to vehicle market conditions |
| 236115 | New Single-Family Housing Construction | ELEVATED RISK | Cyclical industry; revenue dependent on real estate market; speculative element on spec construction |
| 236220 | Commercial and Institutional Building Construction | MODERATE-ELEVATED | Project-based revenue; backlog concentration; bonding and insurance requirements add cost |
| 561720 | Janitorial Services | ELEVATED RISK | High labor intensity; thin margins; contract concentration risk; difficult to build scalable business value |
| 812112 | Beauty Salons | ELEVATED RISK | Stylist-dependent revenue; client relationships personal not business; low barriers to competition; limited hard collateral |
| 812111 | Barber Shops | ELEVATED RISK | As above; low average transaction value; client portability risk |
| 812310 | Coin-Operated Laundries and Drycleaners | MODERATE | Equipment-heavy (can serve as collateral); requires high utilization rates to service debt |
| 713940 | Fitness and Recreational Sports Centers (gyms) | ELEVATED RISK | High fixed lease costs; membership churn; capital-intensive equipment; COVID-type disruption risk |

## Industry Risk Tier Mapping

Use this mapping to assign the initial risk tier for pricing purposes before detailed financial underwriting is completed. This is a starting point; compensating factors (operating history, DSCR, collateral) can move an applicant up or down one tier.

### Tier 1 — Low Risk

Industries with stable, recurring revenue, established regulatory frameworks, strong asset bases, and low historical SBA default rates.

- Healthcare offices and clinics (NAICS 621xxx) — recurring patient revenue, insurance reimbursement stability
- Professional, scientific, and technical services (NAICS 541xxx) — low overhead, recurring client relationships
- Manufacturing (NAICS 31xxx–33xxx) — equipment-backed collateral, supply contract stability
- Wholesale trade (NAICS 42xxx) — inventory collateral, established supply chains
- Established retail (NAICS 44xxx–45xxx, 5+ years in business) — proven local market position
- Technology companies / software (NAICS 511210, 541512, 541519) — recurring SaaS revenue, low capital intensity
- Educational services — private (NAICS 611xxx) — tuition revenue, community need stability

### Tier 2 — Moderate Risk

Industries with generally sound fundamentals but meaningful revenue variability or operational complexity.

- Full-service restaurants with 3+ years of operating history (NAICS 722511)
- General contractors and construction (NAICS 236xxx, 237xxx) — project backlog must be verified
- Transportation and warehousing (NAICS 48xxx–49xxx) — fuel cost exposure; assess fleet age
- Auto repair and maintenance (NAICS 811111) — recurring need but technician-dependent
- Retail with e-commerce presence (NAICS 44xxx–45xxx, 2–5 years) — online channel mitigates foot traffic risk
- Personal care services — established (NAICS 812xxx, 5+ years) — loyal clientele is compensating factor

### Tier 3 — Elevated Risk

Industries with high revenue volatility, limited hard collateral, short track records in the current form, or structural characteristics that increase default probability.

- Event planning and promotion (NAICS 711xxx, 561920) — all operating histories
- New restaurants under 3 years (NAICS 722511, 722513)
- Bars and drinking establishments (NAICS 722410)
- Beauty salons and barbershops under 5 years (NAICS 812111, 812112)
- Entertainment venues (NAICS 713xxx, excluding prohibited gambling)
- Seasonal businesses where >50% of revenue occurs in fewer than 4 months
- Startup businesses (any industry, under 2 years) — automatically Tier 3 minimum
- Taxi/rideshare and transportation network companies (NAICS 485310)

**Underwriting rule**: A Tier 3 industry classification alone does not disqualify a loan. The applicant must demonstrate compensating factors: DSCR ≥ 1.5x (rather than the standard 1.25x minimum), collateral coverage ≥ 75% of loan amount, and a minimum of 3 years of operating history to receive an ELIGIBLE recommendation. Tier 3 applicants meeting only minimum thresholds should receive a REVIEW recommendation pending manual credit review.
